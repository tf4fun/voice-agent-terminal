Original font hashes, converter version, character inventory and generated
payload identities are recorded in manifest.json. Reproduction is implemented
by tools/generate_memo_fonts.py in the repository root.

The 16 px Bold chat inventory includes all 6,763 GB2312 Chinese characters,
including level-two characters such as U+6E25, and common Chinese/typographic
punctuation including curly quotes, en/em dashes and ellipsis. The 20 px Regular
UI subset includes these punctuation glyphs and falls
back to the chat font. The 16 px Regular metadata subset covers printable ASCII
for dates and battery percentages. Each output has a separate inventory and
payload hash in manifest.json. Font bitmaps and tables are const Flash resources.
